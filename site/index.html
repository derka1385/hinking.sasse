What we do

Five disciplines

01 / 05

  1. 01

    Hiking

    Day trails on Sörmlandsleden and Roslagsleden, an hour from the city. Every other weekend, all year.

    Open to allAll year

  2. 02

    Climbing

    Indoor walls through the dark months, granite crags south of Stockholm from May. Belay courses for beginners.

    Course providedAll year

  3. 03

    Alpine

    Glaciers, ropes and early starts. Always with certified mountain guides, never more than six to a rope team.

    Experience requiredJuly – September

  4. 04

    Winter

    Snowshoes, ski touring and frozen lakes. Short days, long light, very cold coffee.

    Open to allDecember – April

  5. 05

    Expeditions

    Several days, far north. Night trains, mountain huts, no signal. Two or three a year, numbered.

    Selected by dossierTwice a year

05

The club

Part of SASSE
× SASSE Stockholm School of Economics

A student club of SASSE, the Student Association at the Stockholm School of Economics. Run by students, open to every member, organised with the same care as the rest of the school.

Who

Every SSE student with a SASSE membership. Bachelor, Master, exchange.

Level

Most trips need no experience. The harder ones say so in the dossier, and we will tell you honestly if one is not for you yet.

Safety

Certified guides on glaciers and alpine terrain. Small groups. The equipment list arrives a week before departure.

  1. IThe group sets the pace.
  2. IIThe summit is optional. Coming back is not.
  3. IIILeave no trace.
