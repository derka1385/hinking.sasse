02Route24 km · +961 m

The way up.

  1. Day 1 · 10.3 km

    Up the Abisko valley

    Abisko Turiststation → Abiskojaure

    Off the night train at eleven. Through the canyon and up Kungsleden along the river to the hut at Abiskojaure.

  2. Day 2 · 6.4 km

    Across the open fell

    Abiskojaure → Njullá shoulder

    Leave the trail. North over open fell above the birches, camp or hut depending on the snow line.

  3. Day 3 · 7.1 km

    Njullá, then the train

    Njullá → Abisko Turiststation

    The ridge to the top at 1,169 m with the guide, down to the station for the evening train south.

05Packing list10 items

What to bring.

  • Shell jacket and trousers
  • Insulated jacket
  • Two base layers, wool
  • Hat, buff, two pairs of gloves
  • Broken-in boots, gaiters
  • Headlamp, spare batteries
  • Sleeping bag to −5 °C
  • Thermos, 1 l water
  • Sit mat, spoon, mug
  • Map and compass, the club brings the rest
